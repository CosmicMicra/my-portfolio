so, that's my job, more or less

    
Opening things up before you trust them.

    

      
I work at Rapticore, on something we call Overwatch. The short version: before a package gets installed, Overwatch opens it up and checks what it really does.

      
If it's up to something, like reaching for your keys or quietly talking to a server it has no business talking to, it stops right there and explains why, so a person gets the final say.

      
What I like about it is that it's half detective work, half engineering. And now that AI tools install things on their own, it matters more than it used to.
