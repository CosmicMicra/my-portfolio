about

I did my master's in Computer Science at Santa Clara University, focusing on AI and machine learning. Before that I studied computer engineering at Pune University and interned as an ML engineer, building anomaly detection for system logs.

Most of my projects sit where machine learning meets real infrastructure: retrieval systems, Kubernetes monitoring, security tooling. I also like hackathon projects that help people directly, like a resource map for unhoused neighbors in San Francisco.

I like to understand how a system works before I make it smarter.
